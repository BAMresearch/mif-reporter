Terms of Use

            
General guidance for using this reporting tool.

          

          
        

        

          
This tool is provided as a practical support utility for preparing mechanochemical reporting data and related scheme graphics.

          
Please review all generated outputs carefully before use, sharing, submission, or publication. You remain responsible for verifying correctness, completeness, and suitability for your intended purpose.

          
No warranty is given that the generated content is free from errors, formatting issues, or interpretation issues. Generated exports should be treated as working drafts unless independently checked.

          
This tool may change over time, including its fields, formatting, export structure, and generated scheme output.

        

      

    

    

      

        

          

            
Privacy Policy

            
General information on data handling in this tool.

          

          
        

        

          
This tool is intended to process the information you enter directly in the browser session and to generate files for download based on that input.

          
Data entered into the form may be included in generated MIF, JSON, SVG, and PNG exports.

          
If this tool is used in a local or browser-only setup, entered data typically remains in your local session unless you intentionally export, transmit, or share it through other systems or services.

          
Please do not enter confidential, personal, proprietary, or otherwise sensitive information unless you intend to process and handle that information in your own usage environment.
